Mechanical Fasteners Thread Feature Hexagon Head Assembly Alignment

Nut vs. Bolt Comparison

Feature Bolt (External Thread) Nut (Internal Thread)
Threading External helical threads on cylindrical shank Internal helical threads inside central hole
Head/Shape Integrated head (Hexagonal) with extended shank Hexagonal or square block with hollow core
Function Inserted through aligned unthreaded clearance holes Screwed onto the bolt shank to clamp components

Day 05

Gear Design & Dynamic Motion Simulation

Explored mechanical power transmission by designing spur gear profiles in Autodesk Fusion. Mastered pitch diameter calculations, tooth profile sketching, circular patterning of gear teeth, and setting up Motion Links and Revolute Joints to simulate gear rotation and mesh interactions.

Spur Gear Design Revolute Joints Motion Link Rotational Simulation

Gear Modeling & Motion Workflow

  • Gear Tooth Profile: Sketched pitch circles, root circles, and addendum/dedendum profiles using precise angular parameters.
  • Circular Patterning: Arrayed individual tooth profiles around the central pitch axis to generate the full gear tooth wheel.
  • Assembly Joints: Applied Revolute Joints to fix gear centers while allowing 360° axial rotation.
  • Rotational Motion Link: Established a 1:1 (or proportional) gear ratio motion link to simulate realistic, synchronized tooth meshing without interference.
Day 06

Product Design: Custom Button Component

Began modeling a custom 3D button component in Autodesk Fusion. Focused on applying smooth ergonomic contours using Fillets and Chamfers, creating thread/attachment geometries, and preparing multi-body components for assembly modeling and rendering.

3D Product Modeling Fillet & Chamfer Component Design Surface Contouring

Button Design Workflow & Techniques

  • Base Profile Sketching: Defined the fundamental outer diameter, inner recess, and functional pin geometry.
  • Primary Extrusion: Created the main cylindrical or shaped solid body using Extrude commands.
  • Ergonomic Detailing: Applied variable radii Fillets and Chamfers to edge profiles to ensure smooth tactile feel.
  • Component Preparation: Organized bodies into standalone components ready for assembly joints and material rendering.
