3D Printing & Designing Progress

Full Assembly Project

Automated Door Locking System Enclosure

Designed and completed a fully functional 3D printable enclosure for an automated Door Locking System from scratch. This project consolidated key additive manufacturing concepts, enabling independent mechanical design and Rapid Prototyping for real-world IoT applications.

3D Printing / FDM Rapid Prototyping Tolerance & Fit 3D Enclosure Assembly

Core Learnings & Technical Skills

  • Independent Mechanical Design: Modeled custom mounting structures, hinges, and snap-fit/screw-down housings to fit electronic modules precisely.
  • Electronics Integration: Integrated precise mounting locations for an ESP8266 Wi-Fi board, SG90 servo motor, and 18650 battery power pack.
  • 3D Model Export & Slicing: Mastered exporting solid models into .STL / .3MF formats and configuring slicing parameters (Infill %, Layer Height, Print Speed) for physical production.
